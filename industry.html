Pillar 2: Clean & Inclusive Industry

Clean Manufacturing & SME Support (Targets 9.2, 9.3 & 9.4)

Manufacturing Trends & Clean Energy Deployment

Globally, manufacturing and trade have rebounded since the pandemic, though ongoing supply chain disruptions, trade tensions and geopolitical instability continue to drive up costs. Global manufacturing value added (MVA) per capita rose by 18.1% between 2015 and 2025. Global manufacturing growth slowed down to 2.7 per cent in 2022, down from 7.4 per cent in 2021, and rebounded to 3 per cent in 2024 and rose a further 2.7 per cent in 2025. The share of manufacturing employment in total employment was 13.7 per cent in 2025, down from 14.3 per cent in 2015.

In 2024, global carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions from fuel combustion and industrial processes surged to a new all-time high of 38.1 gigatons, reverting to a decade-long trend of decoupling emissions and economic growth.

On a positive note, clean energy deployment is actively helping to limit emissions. Since 2019, the rapid expansion of solar power, wind energy, nuclear power, electric vehicles, and heat pumps prevented an estimated 3 gigatons of CO2 emissions in 2025 alone—representing roughly 8% of global energy-related emissions.

How Can We Help?

You can take action by establishing clear standards and promoting regulations that ensure your own workplace projects are sustainably managed.

Collaborating with NGOs and the public sector is another powerful way to promote sustainable growth within developing countries.

Finally, consider how local industries impact your daily well-being, and use your voice on social media to push policymakers to prioritize the SDGs.
